WowMacCraft bundles these programs. Their licences are in this folder.

- Benilla (the WoW client, wow/benilla), MIT or Apache-2.0, built for macOS from
  https://github.com/samwhosung/benilla at commit 2e82d34c, with WowMacCraft's WowCraft
  module added (crates/benilla-app/src/wowcraft in the WowMacCraft source).
- vmangos (the WoW server, server/), GPL-2.0. Built unmodified for macOS from
  https://github.com/vmangos/core at commit 4b3d241; that is its complete source.
- MariaDB 10.11.19 (the database, mariadb/), GPL-2.0. Built unmodified for macOS from
  https://archive.mariadb.org/mariadb-10.11.19/source/mariadb-10.11.19.tar.gz.
- Prism Launcher 11.1.1 for macOS (prism/), GPL-3.0, the official build, unmodified. Source:
  https://github.com/PrismLauncher/PrismLauncher (tag 11.1.1).
- Fabric API, Apache-2.0: https://github.com/FabricMC/fabric
- e4mc, MIT: https://github.com/vgskye/e4mc-minecraft-architectury
- Lithium, LGPL-3.0: https://github.com/CaffeineMC/lithium
- FerriteCore, MIT: https://github.com/malte0811/FerriteCore
- The WowCraft Minecraft mod (a fork of SkyCraft), MIT, with its link to the WoW client moved
  from Windows' kernel32 to macOS' POSIX shared memory (dev/skycraft/link/MacNative.class).
- The WowCraft launcher, MIT, ported to macOS (WowMacCraft.app).

No Blizzard or Mojang files are included. The server's map data is extracted on your Mac from
your own WoW 1.12 client the first time you press Play.
